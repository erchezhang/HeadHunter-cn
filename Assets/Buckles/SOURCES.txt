Buckles (HH-161), made by us: the website's buckle design (head-hunter-web
docs/website/buckles.html, drawn in code as SVG) rendered without the name ribbon, 256x128,
32-bit TGA with alpha, the plate at x 40..216 (Sync/Buckles.lua CROP).

<id>.tga             one picture per buckle, the website's Buckle enum (17)
plate_<quality>.tga  the plate and gem of a quality without an emblem (uncommon, rare,
                     epic, legendary, awarded), for a buckle this version does not know

The emblems are icons from Lucide (https://lucide.dev, ISC licence), raised and lit in
the design.
